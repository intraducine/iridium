# Contributing

Contributions to this fork are accepted under **GPL-3.0-or-later**. By
submitting a change you certify that you wrote it or otherwise have the right
to submit it under that licence, per the
[Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/), and you
sign off your commits:

    git commit -s

Do not remove or alter upstream copyright lines. See `LICENSE-MADEIRA.md` for
why the licence notices refer to the ordinary GPL rather than the Lesser GPL.

Changes intended for upstream Wine should be sent to WineHQ against upstream,
which remains LGPL-2.1-or-later.
