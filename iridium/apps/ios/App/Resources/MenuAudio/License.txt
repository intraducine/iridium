Iridium uses ten selected clips for menu feedback. They are converted to mono
16-bit PCM with excess silence and very low tails trimmed, short edge fades,
lower levels, and file metadata removed. Original pitch and timbre are retained.
source.json records the original archive, source files, output hashes and edits.
